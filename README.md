# 📊 Superstore Sales Analysis Using Python

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing Superstore sales data using Python to understand sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, product performance, and regional trends.

The project follows a data analytics workflow, including data understanding, data preparation, exploratory data analysis (EDA), and visualization. It aims to identify business trends and explore factors that influence sales and profitability.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales, profit, and quantity sold.
- Explore sales performance across different time periods.
- Evaluate profitability across product categories and sub-categories.
- Identify high-performing and loss-making products.
- Understand customer purchasing patterns and segment-level discounts.
- Compare sales and profitability across regions and states.
- Analyze shipping methods and shipping duration.
- Investigate the relationship between discounts and profitability.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Python** — Data analysis
- **Pandas** — Data manipulation and analysis
- **NumPy** — Numerical operations
- **Matplotlib** — Data visualization
- **Jupyter Notebook** — Interactive analysis

## 🔍 Project Workflow

### 1. Data Understanding & Preparation
- Loaded and explored the dataset.
- Examined dataset structure, columns, and statistical summaries.
- Checked for duplicate records and missing values.
- Converted order and shipping dates into datetime format.
- Extracted year, month, quarter, and day-of-week information.

### 2. Sales Performance Analysis
- Calculated total sales and quantity sold.
- Analyzed sales performance across years, months, quarters, and weekdays.
- Examined sales trends using line charts.
- Calculated average sales per order.

### 3. Profitability Analysis
- Calculated total profit and overall profit margin.
- Compared profitability across product categories and sub-categories.
- Identified products with high profits and significant losses.
- Examined profitability across states and regions.

### 4. Product Analysis
- Identified products generating the highest sales revenue.
- Analyzed the most frequently sold products.
- Compared product-level sales and profitability.

### 5. Customer Analysis
- Examined customer purchasing patterns.
- Identified repeat customers.
- Compared discount levels across customer segments.

### 6. Regional Analysis
- Evaluated sales and profit performance across geographical regions.
- Investigated states experiencing the largest losses.
- Explored differences between sales performance and profitability.

### 7. Shipping Analysis
- Examined the popularity of different shipping methods.
- Compared average shipping duration across shipping modes.
- Analyzed profit across shipping methods.

### 8. Discount Analysis
- Calculated the overall average discount.
- Examined profitability at different discount levels.
- Investigated how discounting relates to profit and potential losses.
